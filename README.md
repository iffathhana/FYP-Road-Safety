# FYP Road Safety
The study aims to reduce traffic violations in Sri Lanka by reducing corruption through a system comprising a centralized server, edge server, and fine management system, which can adapt to specific traffic regulations.
The state of the road, traffic, accidents, speed, and other factors all affect how convenient it is to travel. In Sri Lanka, motor traffic violations are the most prevalent kind of infraction. Because there have been so many infractions, the government and police department of Sri Lanka have had a difficult time controlling and stopping them. Road users' lack of self-control and attitude contributes to traffic congestion and can lead to traffic violations. In addition to reducing traffic violations, this study aims to lessen roadside corruption and nepotism. This system was created using three distinct models: centralized server, edge server, and fine management system. The purpose of the Python-developed Edge server is to identify traffic infractions like parking, single- and double-line cutting, one-way and U-turns, and more in real time. This is done in accordance with the ever-changing rules and restrictions set by the traffic police administrator through the fine management system, which supports a platform that can be customized using canvas design tools. When an edger server detects a violation, the centralized server's operations are in charge of identifying the vehicle's license plate. This triggers the driver to get an email or SMS for additional fine management and payment assistance from the fine management system. In the event that the system is unable to correct the car number plate, the online application managing server is in charge of monitoring and notifying the government office of any violations. The system offers a highly adaptable platform, which sets it apart from the competition. This flexibility enables the development of particular regulations for various traffic situations, such as establishing parameters for the detection of traffic infractions according to vehicle and road regulations.
